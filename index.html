Quick help

File formats & folder access

  • Photos. Choose JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP or BMP. Convert HEIC or HEIF photos to JPEG or PNG first.
  • Folders.
  • ZIP files. Use UTF-8 or ASCII filenames. Limits are 50,000 entries, 1 GiB per archive, 512 MiB per file and 2 GiB total when extracted.

Labeling tools

  1. Choose Draw and a New box class. Drag anywhere on the image, including inside an existing box.
  2. Choose Edit, then tap a box or its row. Drag inside to move it, drag a corner to resize it, or use Change selected box class.
  3. To leave out a blurry, duplicate or wrong photo, select × on its thumbnail. The original file stays on your device.
  4. Background photos are optional. To include one in the training ZIP, open Photo options, choose Confirm no object, then choose Save.
  5. Boxes smaller than 3 original-image pixels are rejected.
  6. On an unlabeled photo, Suggest boxes in the toolbar can propose boxes and classes. Change a class beside its box, check for missing objects, then choose Save reviewed boxes.
  7. Choose Save before moving to the next photo. Finish & exit returns to review. Unsaved boxes stay in your workspace as a draft.

Shortcuts: A/← previous, D/→ next, 1–9 classes, Delete remove box, Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo, Ctrl/Cmd+S download draft, Esc cancel/deselect.

Resume last dataset restores the latest workspace in this browser when local storage is available. Download draft ZIP keeps a portable copy of saved labels for Open a ZIP.
